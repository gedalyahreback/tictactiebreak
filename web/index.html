BUILT FOR ONE MORE ROUND.
A FAMILIAR GAME. AN UNEXPECTED TWIST.

Break the tie.

  1. Take turns. Make your mark.

    Place your symbol in an empty square. Use a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter.

  2. Line them up.

    Connect the required number horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

  3. Full board? Keep going.

    If nobody wins, the board expands. The board grows on every side, keeping all existing marks centered. The next player takes their turn. Every expansion increases the winning length by 2: 3 in a row becomes 5, then 7.

Quick play starts at 3 × 3 with 3 to win, then expands to 5 × 5 with 5 to win. Custom games support 2–5 players, larger starting boards, and a choice of adding 1 or 2 rows and columns on every side for 2–4 players. Five-player games always add 3 on every side. Every expansion increases the winning length by 2, regardless of player count. Play together on one device, or challenge the computer.
